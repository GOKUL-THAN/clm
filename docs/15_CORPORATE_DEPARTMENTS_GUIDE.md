# CLM-SPEC-15: CORPORATE DEPARTMENTS OPERATIONAL GUIDE
## Capabilities, Workflows, and Agreement Types Across 14 Enterprise Departments

**Version:** 3.4.0-Enterprise  
**Document ID:** CLM-CORP-015  
**Classification:** Enterprise Operational Standard  

---

### 1. Departmental Architecture & Cross-Functional Governance

Enterprise CLM is not solely a Legal department tool; it is the commercial nervous system of the organization. The platform provides tailored capabilities, specialized contract types, and dedicated approval workflows across 14 corporate departments.

---

### 2. Detailed Departmental Capabilities & Contract Portfolio

| # | Department | Primary Contracts Managed | Specialized Enterprise Capabilities |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **HR** | Offer letters, Executive Employment, NDAs, Contractor Agreements | Employee onboarding intake, non-compete clause tracking, automatic notification of visa/contract renewal dates. |
| **2** | **Finance** | Credit facilities, Debt instruments, Billing terms, Revenue recognition | Revenue leakage detection, milestone payment reconciliation, dynamic discount tracking, currency risk exposure. |
| **3** | **Procurement** | Master Supply Agreements (MSA), Purchase Orders, Sourcing RFPs | Three-way matching (PO/Receipt/Invoice), supplier risk tiering (Tier 1-3), diversity tracking, spend analytics. |
| **4** | **Sales** | Customer MSAs, Order Forms, Reseller Agreements, SLA Addenda | Deal Desk review queues, discounting threshold governance, customer auto-renewal notifications, revenue recognition flags. |
| **5** | **Legal** | Commercial dispute settlements, IP licensing, Outside Counsel Retainers | Litigation hold preservation, standard clause playbook management, fallback clause guidance, external redline reviews. |
| **6** | **IT** | Software Licenses, SaaS Subscriptions, Cloud Infrastructure (IaaS/PaaS) | License seat true-up tracking, cloud usage expenditure caps, hardware lease expiration calendars, vendor API SLAs. |
| **7** | **Security** | Data Processing Agreements (DPA), Security Addenda, Penetration Testing | Vendor SOC 2 / ISO 27001 certificate verification, breach notification SLA tracking (72-hr GDPR), security scorecards. |
| **8** | **Operations** | Logistics Agreements, Equipment Maintenance, Service Level Agreements | Operational deliverable tracking, uptime SLA monitoring, service credit penalty calculations, milestone signoffs. |
| **9** | **Manufacturing** | Tooling Contracts, OEM Supply Agreements, Product Warranty Terms | Manufacturing defect indemnification, batch delivery milestones, raw material price variance formulas, RMA policies. |
| **10** | **Facilities** | Commercial Real Estate Leases, Janitorial Contracts, HVAC Servicing | Lease renewal/termination critical notice dates, square footage escalation terms, tenant improvement allowances. |
| **11** | **Marketing** | Agency Retainers, Celebrity Sponsorships, Event Partnerships | Marketing deliverable acceptance workflows, intellectual property / trademark licensing, social media conduct clauses. |
| **12** | **Compliance** | Regulatory Filings, Statutory Audit Agreements, Export Controls | Anti-bribery (FCPA / UK Bribery Act) attestations, export license compliance, statutory audit evidence collection. |
| **13** | **Administration** | Corporate Policies, Delegation of Authority (DOA), Power of Attorney | DOA threshold matrix enforcement, signatory permission registries, board resolution tracking, travel policy agreements. |
| **14** | **Executive Mgmt**| Strategic Joint Ventures, M&A NDAs, Board Charters, Investor Rights | High-level portfolio risk heatmaps, enterprise valuation metrics, consolidated contract liability visibility. |

---

### 3. Departmental Navigation in Admin Portal

The Administrative Portal (`web/admin-portal/`) provides a centralized **Corporate Departments (14)** hub view allowing cross-functional leaders to filter agreements, monitor active requests, and track departmental obligations in a single pane of glass.
